Inspection

How We Check Our Laptops

Every laptop gets its own inspection record. You see the result on the product page, including any known marks or defects.

1

Verify the laptop

Check the make, model, serial, and identifying details against the import manifest.

2

Test hardware components

Boot, keyboard, trackpad, screen, ports, battery, Wi-Fi, and storage health.

3

Record condition & defects

Photograph any cosmetic marks and document known issues on the product page.

4

Approve for sale

Only units that pass all four steps are listed. The rest are set aside — not sold.

Support

Frequently Asked Questions

Still have a question?
Are these laptops new or used?
They are authentic used laptops imported from Dubai. Each unit is individually inspected and condition-graded before sale.
How are laptops inspected?
Every laptop passes a four-step inspection: identity verification, hardware testing, condition documentation, and final approval.
What does the condition grade mean?
Grade A = like new with minimal signs of use. Grade B = light wear. Grade C = visible marks documented on the product page.
Is a warranty available?
Warranty is shown per-unit on the product page when it applies. Not all units carry the same warranty terms.
Can I visit the physical store?
Yes — our store address and business hours are listed on the Contact page.
What payment methods are supported?
Cash, bank transfer, bKash, and Nagad. Payment methods are configured by the business.
How does delivery work?
Home delivery is available across Bangladesh. Store pickup is free. Delivery fees are shown at checkout.
What is the return policy?
Return policy is set by the business. Please see our returns page or contact support for details.
